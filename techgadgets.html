Tech Gadgets

These gadgets cover the everyday tech upgrades that tend to get used constantly rather than sitting in a drawer, from streaming to smart home convenience. Each pick is chosen for reliability and broad appeal across different households.

How to choose tech gadgets that get daily use

  • Check your home internet speed before buying a streaming device, since a slow connection can limit even the best hardware.
  • Smart speakers work best when placed centrally in a room rather than tucked in a corner, so plan placement before buying more than one.
  • Portable speakers with water resistance are a safer choice for anyone who plans to use them outdoors or near a pool.
  • Consider what a device already does well before adding a new one, since some smart home features overlap between devices.

Amazon Echo Dot Smart Speaker

Around $30 to $50 Rated near 4.7 out of 5 with a review count in the hundreds of thousands

The Echo Dot is a compact smart speaker with a built in voice assistant that can play music, answer questions, control smart home devices, and set reminders. Its small size makes it easy to place on a nightstand, kitchen counter, or desk.

Pros

Who should buy it. Shoppers building out a smart home, or anyone who wants hands free convenience in a bedroom, kitchen, or office.

Overall recommendation. A dependable, well known entry point into smart home technology, and a strong pick for the tech gadgets category.
