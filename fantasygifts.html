Fantasy Gifts

For readers who fell in love with dragons, dice, and worlds beyond our own, this collection gathers fantasy gifts that hold up to real use, not just a pretty photo. Every pick here works for a first time tabletop player, a lifelong collector, or anyone shopping for the fantasy fan in their life.

How to choose a fantasy gift that actually gets used

  • Think about how the person already engages with fantasy. A dedicated tabletop player will get more use from a rulebook or dice set than from decor alone.
  • Check the material and size before buying a display piece like a statue or figurine, since delicate resin pieces need a safe spot on a shelf or desk.
  • Dice sets and small accessories make excellent add on gifts alongside a larger item like a rulebook or board game.
  • If you are shopping for a beginner, a core rulebook is almost always a safer starting point than an expansion or accessory.

Fantasy staple Great first purchase

Dungeons and Dragons Player's Handbook, Fifth Edition Core Rulebook

Around $30 to $40 Consistently rated near 4.8 out of 5 by tens of thousands of players

The Player's Handbook is the core rulebook every Dungeons and Dragons player needs to build a character and understand the rules of play. It covers races, classes, spells, equipment, and the core mechanics that guide a full campaign.

Pros

  • Complete reference for character creation
  • Beautiful artwork throughout the book
  • Works for brand new players and long time dungeon masters alike

Cons

  • Dense reading for someone brand new to tabletop games
  • A separate Dungeon Master Guide is needed to run full campaigns

Who should buy it. Anyone starting their first campaign, or a longtime fan who wants a fresh copy to replace a worn one.

Overall recommendation. A foundational purchase for the fantasy and tabletop crowd, and one of the easiest fantasy themed gifts to recommend with confidence.

Budget friendly Easy add on gift

Polyhedral Dice Set With Carrying Pouch

Around $10 to $20 Frequently rated 4.6 out of 5 or higher across thousands of reviews

A complete seven piece polyhedral dice set gives every tabletop player the tools they need for Dungeons and Dragons, Pathfinder, and other role playing games. Sets typically arrive in a drawstring or velvet pouch that keeps the pieces organized between sessions.

Pros

  • Inexpensive and easy to gift
  • Comes in dozens of colors and finishes
  • A drawstring bag keeps every die organized

Cons

  • Cheaper acrylic sets can feel light compared to metal or resin dice
  • Numbers on some budget sets can be harder to read in low light

Who should buy it. Anyone who plays tabletop games regularly, or a thoughtful stocking stuffer for a fantasy fan.

Overall recommendation. A small purchase that consistently earns high marks for value, making it an easy add on next to a rulebook or board game.
