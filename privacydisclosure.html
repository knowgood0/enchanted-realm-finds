Privacy and Affiliate Disclosure

This page explains how Enchanted Realm Finds handles your information and how our affiliate links work. We believe you should always know how a site earns money before you trust its recommendations.

Affiliate disclosure

When you click a Buy on Amazon button on this site and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.

Our reviews reflect an honest assessment based on publicly available product information and widely reported customer feedback. Being part of an affiliate program does not change our approach to what we choose to feature or how we describe it.

Information we collect

This site does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name or payment details directly. Standard web hosting logs may record general technical information, like browser type or approximate region, for security and performance purposes.

If analytics tools are added in the future, they would be used only to understand which pages are helpful to visitors, and never to sell personal data to third parties.

Cookies

Amazon and other third party services linked from this site may place their own cookies once you leave Enchanted Realm Finds. Those cookies are controlled by the third party site, not by us, and are covered by that site's own privacy policy.

Third party links

Every Buy on Amazon button on this site leads to a listing on Amazon.com. We do our best to link each button to the correct, current listing, but Amazon may change product availability, pricing or listing details at any time. Always confirm the details on the Amazon product page before completing a purchase.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time as the site grows. Please check back occasionally to stay informed of how we operate.
