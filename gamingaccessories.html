How to pick the right gaming mouse

  • Decide between wired and wireless first. Wired mice remove any chance of input lag, while modern wireless mice like the ones featured here are fast enough for most players.
  • Consider hand size and grip style, since a mouse that fits a palm grip may feel awkward for a fingertip grip player.
  • Weight matters more than most shoppers expect. Lighter mice suit fast, flick heavy games, while heavier mice can feel more stable for precise aiming.
  • Programmable buttons are worth paying extra for if you play games that benefit from quick macros or shortcuts.
