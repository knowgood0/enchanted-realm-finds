Home and Desk Upgrades

A better desk changes how a whole day feels, whether that means more room for a dual monitor setup or a splash of color from LED lighting. These picks focus on upgrades that are simple to install and genuinely improve comfort or organization.

How to plan a desk upgrade that fits your space

  • Measure your desk depth and edge thickness before ordering a clamp mounted stand or riser, since not every desk supports every mount type.
  • Start with the upgrade that solves your biggest daily frustration, whether that is limited surface space, poor lighting, or sitting too long without a break.
  • Cable management is easier to plan before new hardware arrives, so consider routing power and monitor cables as part of the setup.
  • Lighting upgrades are one of the fastest, lowest cost ways to change the feel of a room or desk without any tools.
