Logitech G305 LIGHTSPEED review: Affordable Wireless Precision for Casual and Competitive Play

The Logitech G305 LIGHTSPEED wireless gaming mouse is aimed at players who want the reliability of a wired sensor without the cable clutter. It’s a good fit for budget‑conscious gamers, streamers who value a tidy desk, and anyone who prefers a lightweight mouse that works well with claw or fingertip grips. Shoppers typically find it on Amazon for roughly ~$25‑$40, making it one of the cheapest LIGHTSPEED‑enabled options available.

Quick verdict

The G305 delivers the core advantages of Logitech’s LIGHTSPEED wireless tech—sub‑millisecond latency and a solid HERO sensor—at a price that undercuts most competitors. Its tiny footprint and 99‑gram weight keep hand fatigue low, while a single AA battery can power the mouse for months. If you’re comfortable with a non‑rechargeable battery and don’t need flashy RGB, the G305 is a practical, performance‑first choice.

What we like – Logitech G305 LIGHTSPEED review

  • LIGHTSPEED wireless performance – The proprietary 2.4 GHz connection offers wired‑level responsiveness, which is essential for fast‑paced shooters and MOBA titles.
  • HERO sensor – Logitech's HERO sensor delivers precise, consistent tracking with no smoothing or acceleration at the sensitivities most players actually use.
  • Long battery life on one AA – In typical use, the mouse can run for 250 hours or more before the battery needs replacement, meaning you won’t be swapping cells every week.
  • Lightweight, clean shape – At 99 g and with a symmetrical silhouette, the G305 feels natural for claw and fingertip grips, and the minimal button layout reduces accidental clicks.

What could be better – Logitech G305 LIGHTSPEED review

  • AA battery instead of built‑in rechargeable pack – While the long life is a plus, some users prefer the convenience of a USB‑charged internal battery.
  • No RGB lighting – The mouse is deliberately understated, which may disappoint gamers who like customizable lighting zones.
  • Basic scroll wheel – The wheel lacks a free‑spin mode and feels a bit stiff compared with higher‑end models, though it still functions reliably for most games.

Who should buy it / who should skip it

Buy it if you need a wireless mouse that performs like a wired device without breaking the bank. The G305 is especially appealing for players with small to medium‑sized hands who favor a light, low‑profile mouse for rapid movements. Its battery longevity and plug‑and‑play setup also make it a solid pick for streamers who want a tidy desk and minimal cable management.

Skip it if you prioritize built‑in RGB
