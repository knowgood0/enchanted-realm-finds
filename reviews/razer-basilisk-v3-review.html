Razer Basilisk V3 review: A solid wired gaming mouse for long sessions

The Razer Basilisk V3 is aimed at gamers who spend hours at the desk and want a responsive, comfortable mouse without the hassle of batteries. If you prefer a wired connection for reliable low‑latency input and enjoy a bit of personal flair with RGB lighting, this mouse fits the bill. Below is a Razer Basilisk V3 review that walks through the key points you need to decide.

Quick verdict – Razer Basilisk V3 review

The Basilisk V3 delivers the essentials that competitive and casual gamers look for: a fast optical switch, an ergonomic shape that stays comfortable during marathon play, and a tilting scroll wheel for precise control. Its wired USB connection keeps input delay to a minimum, and the customizable RGB adds a touch of personality. At a price of ~$40‑$70, it offers good value for a mid‑range gaming mouse.

What we like – Razer Basilisk V3 review

  • Fast optical switch – clicks register instantly, giving you the responsiveness needed for fast‑paced games.
  • Tilting scroll wheel – adds a secondary tilt function for quick weapon switching or page navigation without sacrificing the primary scroll feel.
  • Customizable RGB lighting – lets you match the mouse to your rig’s color scheme or create subtle ambience for a more immersive setup.
  • Ergonomic shape built for long sessions – the contouring supports your hand comfortably, reducing fatigue during extended play.

What could be better

  • The wired design, while great for latency, limits flexibility for users who prefer a clutter‑free desk.
  • RGB lighting can draw extra power, which may be a consideration for users with strict power budgets.
  • The mouse does not include additional programmable buttons beyond the core set, which some high‑level competitive players might miss.

Who should buy it / who should skip it – Razer Basilisk V3 review

Buy it if you value a reliable wired connection and need a mouse that stays comfortable through long gaming sessions. The ergonomic design and tilting scroll wheel make it especially suitable for FPS and MOBA titles where quick reactions and precise scrolling matter. If you also enjoy tweaking lighting effects to match a themed setup, the Basilisk V3’s RGB will fit right in.

Skip it if you prioritize wireless freedom or need a mouse with a larger suite of programmable buttons for complex macros. Players who demand a high‑end DPI sensor or on‑the‑fly weight adjustments may also look elsewhere, as those features are not highlighted in this model.

FAQ

Q: Does the Razer Basilisk V3 need batteries?

A: No, it connects via a wired USB link, so it draws power directly from the computer and never needs batteries.

Q: Can the RGB lighting be turned off?

A: Yes, the lighting is fully customizable, allowing you to disable it entirely if you prefer a more understated look.

Q: Is the mouse compatible with both Windows and macOS?

A: The Basilisk V3 works with the major desktop operating systems, including Windows and macOS, using the standard USB connection.
